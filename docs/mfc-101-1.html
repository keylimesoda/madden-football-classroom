Shells

Learn the first pre-snap concept: one-high and two-high safety shells. You will not call coverages yet. You will learn the visual structure that begins the rest of the read.

Learning objective

See the shell before you solve the play.

Deep-safety alignment is your first clue because it changes where the defense visibly has help. Learn the vocabulary now; use it later in the complete pre-snap scan.

By the snap, say: “I see one high” or “I see two high.” Then add: “That is a starting shell—not a coverage verdict.”
Base picture

Hold the offense constant.

Before comparing defenses, anchor yourself in a normal offense. This is not five wide: it is 11 personnel from shotgun—one back, one tight end, and three wide receivers.

Base 11 personnel: 1 RB, 1 TE, 3 WR. The QB is in shotgun; the RB is aligned beside him; the TE creates the strong side to the right. This exact offensive picture stays stable while the defensive clue changes.

Two high

Two deep safeties

Both safeties are positioned deep before the snap. Begin by checking the box and the space underneath. Do not announce “Cover 2” just because you can count two safeties.

One high

One middle safety

One safety sits in the deep middle. Begin by locating the extra defender near the line and by noticing outside leverage. Do not announce “Cover 3” or “man” yet.

What it means

A shell is a map, not the territory.

It tells you where the defense starts. The movement after the ball is snapped tells you where the defense ends.

Count deep safeties.

One-high means one; two-high means two. Make this a fast, visual count.

Choose one related question.

With two high, your next question is often “how many are in the box?” With one high, it is often “where is the extra defender and what is the corner leverage?”

Confirm after the snap.

Safety rotation can change the picture. A two-high shell can rotate to one high; a one-high safety can drive into the box. Keep a safe answer ready.

Helpful sentence: “Two high gives me a box-count question.” Unhelpful sentence: “Two high proves Cover 2.”
Madden practice mode · 4 minutes

Build shell recognition first.

  1. Pick one 11-personnel shotgun formation with the RB beside the QB and TE to the strong side.
  2. Run the same call against one-high and two-high defensive looks.
  3. Before snapping, say only: “one high” or “two high.” Do not guess the full coverage.
  4. After the snap, pause replay at one second. Did either safety rotate? Say what changed.
Knowledge check

Two safeties are deep. What can you say with confidence?
