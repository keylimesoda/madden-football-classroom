The Pre-Snap Scan

Learning objective

Bundle the concepts into one calm pre-snap routine.

Now that shell, box count, and leverage have meaning, use them in a fixed order. The goal is a reasonable first plan—not a perfect prediction.

By the snap, say: “Two high. Seven near the box. Off corner to trips. I will check the run count first, then confirm safety rotation and take the safe answer if the picture changes.”
The complete picture

Same formation. Four fast questions.

Nothing here is a guess. Each question uses a visible part of the field and produces one action for the player.

Base 11 personnel: 1 RB, 1 TE, 3 WR. The QB is in shotgun; the RB is aligned beside him; the TE creates the strong side to the right. This exact offensive picture stays stable while the defensive clue changes.

1 · Shell

Where is the deep help?

Two high. That sends you to the box-count question.

2 · Box

Who can affect the run?

Seven near the line. Compare that number to your actual blocking concept.

3 · Leverage

What is your quick outlet?

Off coverage on the trips side suggests cushion—but verify it after the snap.

4 · Plan

What changes your mind?

If safeties rotate or the quick window closes, move to the safe answer instead of forcing the original idea.

The scan

Shell → box → leverage → plan.

Use the exact order every snap until it becomes automatic. The order prevents one flashy clue from crowding out the rest of the picture.

Shell: one high or two high?

Locate the deep safeties. This tells you where to start looking next.

Box: how many are near the line?

Count defenders who can affect the run and compare them with your run concept.

Leverage: what does the nearest defender concede?

See cushion, press, and inside/outside alignment. Choose a concept to inspect—not a throw to force.

Plan: first answer and safe answer.

Name what you will check immediately after the snap and where you go if that answer disappears.

Madden practice mode · 6 minutes

Run the whole scan.

  1. Call an 11-personnel shotgun play with both a run and a quick pass available.
  2. Before every snap, say all four words: shell, box, leverage, plan.
  3. After the snap, make one decision only: stay with the first answer or move to the safe answer.
  4. Use replay to identify the single clue that should have changed your plan.
  5. Repeat until you can scan in five seconds without rushing the read.
