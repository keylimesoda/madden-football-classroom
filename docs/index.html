How a course works

1. See it

Diagram and plain-language explanation.

2. Make a decision

A concrete read, not a trivia term.

3. Run the lab

A short Practice Mode rep you can repeat.

4. Check yourself

One or two recognition questions, then move on.

What MFC is not

Not a list of glitch plays. Not a coaching clinic that assumes you already speak another language. Not a site that gives you a beautiful diagram and then leaves you to figure out what the quarterback should actually do.
